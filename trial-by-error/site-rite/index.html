HOW TO PLAY


You will be given a website page to look at. Something will be wrong with it and it’s up to you to figure out what that thing is. You will be given 3 answers to choose from which narrows down what could be wrong with it, but you will still need to think what is wrong. If you get it right, you earn a Happy horse point (trust me you want those). If you get it wrong, not happy horse points for you!

This is not timed, so please take your time with the questions. More questions will be added later on so please bear with us.
