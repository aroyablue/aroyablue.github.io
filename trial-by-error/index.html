What is Trial By Error?


Trial by Error is a website made with one question in mind; Why is User Experience so important? As the world gets more digital, website and apps become the forefront of what people see as a first impression. And because of this, it is imperative for designers, marketers and businesses to be informed on what makes a website or app so good.

This is where Trial by Error comes into play.

Trial by Error focuses on education and emphasizing the importance of user design by creating simple games that show the struggles people face when design ends up as bad. On top of the games, we believe that the best way to show off good design is by providing an example of a bad design.
